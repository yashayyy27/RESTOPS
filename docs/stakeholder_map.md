# Stakeholder map and operating responsibilities

| Stakeholder | Interest / influence | Decision | Information | Accountability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Managing Director | High / high | Investment and intervention priorities | Growth, margin, ranked opportunities | Sponsor and prioritise |
| Operations Director | High / high | Standards and area support | Store diagnostics and exception trends | Own operating process |
| Area Manager | High / high | Weekly store actions | Staffing, waste, experience and profit | Own intervention worklist |
| Restaurant Manager | High / medium | Rosters and preparation | Hour/daypart demand, waste reasons | Run and document pilots |
| Finance | High / high | Metric approval and reconciliation | Revenue, costs, targets, campaign economics | Approve KPI definitions |
| Marketing / Loyalty | High / medium | Offers and retention | Contribution, cohorts, customer segments | Own controlled experiments |
| Workforce Planning | High / medium | Staffing plan | Forecasts, capacity assumptions, service guardrails | Review demand and overrides |
| Data Analyst | High / medium | Data quality and model selection | Exceptions, contracts, evaluation | Refresh, audit and explain |

## Consultation plan

Finance validates cost and KPI definitions. Managers validate meal peaks and
capacity assumptions. Marketing reviews campaign costs and control-store
selection. Area Managers assess whether the proposed worklist supports weekly
decisions. All consultation is proposed for a real implementation; no interviews
or user testing are claimed in this fictional case study.

## Weekly handoffs

Analyst → Finance: validation and reconciliation exceptions.  
Analyst → Area Manager: ranked stores and diagnostic evidence.  
Area Manager → Restaurant Manager: one or two actions with measurable outcomes.  
Manager → Analyst: intervention dates, roster overrides and contextual explanations.
